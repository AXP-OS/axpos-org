You are considering to contribute to AXP.OS? This is greatly appreciated!

Here some possible ways to support the project:

## Translate

Have you found a typo or pages in bad English? Correct them and create a PR!

Website content: https://code.binbash.rocks/AXP.OS/axpos-org/src/branch/hugo/content

## Write

#### Website

Do you miss a guide or a specific topic within an existing one? Add it and create a PR!

Website content: https://code.binbash.rocks/AXP.OS/axpos-org/src/branch/hugo/content

#### WWW

Spread the word ;) The project needs people like you, developers and anyone interested in more privacy.

Talk about AXP.OS and its apps (e.g. LoveLaceAV, AOSmium etc) e.g. at forums like https://discuss.privacyguides.net, https://xdaforums.com or any other place which fits AXP.OS' usecases.

#### Article / Video creation

You might read specific Android related news, choose your favorite feed and ask them to checkout AXP.OS for an article or video review ;)
