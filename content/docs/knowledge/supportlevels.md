There are currently 2 support levels for AXP.OS available.<br/>
The following describes the differences between including the impact on each AXP.OS [flavor](/Flavors).

### by the AXP team

verified & tested: **by the AXP team** 

|By|Test|Regular|Slim|
|:-:|-|:-:|:-:|
|`AXP team`|AXP.OS was successfully build on the AXP infrastructure and its build systems|**X**|**X**|
|`AXP team`|AXP.OS apps and the OS are properly signed with [AXP keys](/Signatures) and can therefore be verified|**X**|**X**|
|`AXP team`|tested and verified functionality of the OS, apps and any promoted features|**X**|**X**|
|`AXP team`|the whole state (including bootloader lock where applicable) is based on our **own** test results|**X**|**X**|
|`AXP team`|**full** support (as we own that device)|**X**|**X**|
|`AXP team`|every update will be tested on a real device before releasing|**X**||

### by the AXP community

verified & tested: **by the AXP community**

|By|Test|Regular|Slim|
|:-:|-|:-:|:-:|
|`AXP team`|AXP.OS was successfully build on the AXP infrastructure and its build systems|**X**|**X**|
|`AXP team`|AXP.OS apps and the OS are properly signed with [AXP keys](/Signatures) and can therefore be verified|**X**|**X**|
|`AXP community`|tested and verified functionality of the OS, apps and any promoted features based on **user reports**|**X**|**X**|
|`AXP community`|the whole state (including bootloader lock where applicable) is based on **user reports**|**X**|**X**|
|`AXP team`|**limited** support as there is no way to re-produce issues (bug reports can still be opened though)|**X**|**X**|
|`N/A`|every update will be tested on a real device before releasing|||
