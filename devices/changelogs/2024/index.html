      AXP.OS🎁 microG updated to v0.3.5.240913
      AXP.OS🪩 signature spoofing fix
      AXP.OS✨ added LG G4 - H815

      Important

      • 💡 microG has received a big change. You will be prompted to choose a location provider once while positon is suggested by them. It can be changed at any time later of course.
      • 💡 A change made by Google required to adapt the Advanced Signature Spoofing patches coming with AXP.OS. All supported devices will receive that fix asap while it will take a while making the adaption itself. Due to the fact that AXP.OS does not use the LineageOS patches for signature spoofing we cannot just use the available ones.

      2024 - October

      💡🥁 IMPORTANT 🥁 💡

      • Google Pixel 4a (sunfish)

      will contain a big change regarding the reserved partition size. usually this shouldn’t be a problem and you won’t see any difference as the dynamic partition handling should just take care of everything. Nevertheless and especially because I cannot test builds on this device ensure you have a full backup of all important data before applying the October update!
